# Clearer video on weak connections
Lost video packets are now re-sent and actually used, so a picture no longer breaks until a whole new one arrives
No more storm of "send a whole new picture" requests: at most one every few seconds, sized to fit the connection
Bitrate control that rides out short bursts of loss, never raises the bitrate while packets are being lost, and remembers what your upload can carry
On a weak connection the frame rate drops before the picture gets blurry; calls start at 720p 30 fps and grow
One friend with a bad connection no longer shrinks everyone's picture (they get the small stream)
A PC that can't keep up asks for a smaller picture; voice is protected first when a connection struggles
After a graphics driver reset, Tandem goes back to the graphics card by itself
Tiles say why a picture is soft or frozen (their connection, protecting it, reconnecting)
# Voice that survives
Friends whose connection loses packets in bursts get each piece of voice carried twice, so it doesn't break up
A 10-second dropout freezes the picture with a "reconnecting" mark and carries on instead of rebuilding the call
The room notices within seconds when someone has really gone
Voices and call sounds play on your headset's Chat side, so its game/chat dial works (SteelSeries Arctis with Sonar, JBL Quantum and similar)
Microphones already cleaned by Sonar, NVIDIA Broadcast, Krisp and similar get Tandem's noise suppression and automatic volume switched off
# Share audio with chosen people
Share your PC's audio with everyone or pick people; right-click someone for "Share my screen audio with only them"
Small circles on your tile show who's hearing it
# Watching
Right-click a person: Full screen (the whole monitor) or Pop out (their own window you can drag anywhere); double-click also goes full screen
Scroll to zoom into whatever your mouse points at, drag to look around
Pencil: draw on someone's screen to circle things; everyone gets their own colour and name, and drawings fade away
# Layout
Fold away Creator Pulse and Messages (button, the handle on the left, or Ctrl+B) so the chat or room gets bigger
New "Tabs on top" option: move the left tabs into the title bar
# Windows
Hover Tandem on the taskbar for Mute, Deafen, Camera and Leave buttons; a red badge when you're muted
Ctrl+Shift+M and Ctrl+Shift+D mute and deafen during calls, even inside a game (change them in Voice settings)
Tray icon with Mute, Deafen, Leave and Quit
# Lighter on your PC
The Studio really rests when you're not using it, and stays light in calls when it isn't on screen
When a game covers Tandem it's nearly off; beside a game friends' video is lighter
Games that aren't in a store library are noticed too (any program filling the screen), plus an "I'm gaming" switch
Duplicate imported OBS scenes and a second background blur are tidied away automatically (a backup is kept)
A busy camera is retried for 30 seconds and Tandem says which app has it
Interrupted updates restart cleanly instead of failing
