# A whole new Tandem, after the concept art
New navigation on the left: Chats, Live Rooms, Studio, Analytics, Creators and Discover, with New Chat and your card at the bottom
Chats: Creator Pulse shows every voice room live at a glance (LIVE when a camera is on), and Messages lists every channel in all your spaces with the last message
Conversations: a bold header with join-by-voice, join-with-camera and members buttons; your messages show a double tick once delivered
The message box has Studio quick actions (switch scenes), a picture button and "Snap your Studio" to share what your Studio shows
Live Rooms, Analytics (your activity, counted only on your PC), Creators (everyone in your spaces) and Discover (spaces, invites, themes) pages
Ten colour themes and a living background with shimmering light sweeps (can be turned off)
A Tandem shortcut on your desktop (made once; delete it and it stays gone)
# Calls that hold up
If the connection or the server blips, Tandem puts you back in your room by itself and the call carries on
Calls keep working while Tandem is minimised: friends who join while you're in a game now connect to you
Unplugging your headset, a Bluetooth drop or a new default device no longer leaves you silent or deaf: Tandem switches over by itself
Minimised for a while, friends stop sending you video nobody is watching; it's back the moment you open Tandem
Slow connections: video steps down to 540p and 360p instead of breaking up, and needs far less upload in a full room
PCs without a graphics-card encoder now adapt too (to the connection and to how fast the PC can encode)
If the graphics card falls behind (a game using it), Tandem catches the delay and restarts the encoder
Dead connections are noticed twice as fast; stalled call setups are rebuilt automatically
Sharper video: friends come in at 720p or more in a normal tile; bitrate follows the picture size
Phones in voice-only or console mode get no video, saving their data
Much lighter during calls: the window redraws only when something changes (it was 30-66 times a second)
# Chat
Unread dots and mention badges are remembered across restarts and reconnects
Your message never silently vanishes: it shows "Sending…" and, if it can't go, "Not sent" with Retry, Edit and Delete
Mentions, sounds and finished uploads work on every page and while minimised; messages arriving while you're in a game stay unread
Messages starting with "/" (like /shrug) are sent as text; only Studio commands are taken as commands
Deleting a channel asks first
# Studio
New Output setting: send and record at 1080p or 720p and 30 or 60 fps (new installs start at 1080p30, much lighter on laptops)
Cameras switch off (light off) when nothing uses them, and no longer flash on at start with cameras off
Web page overlays that aren't on screen stop running, saving memory and CPU
Importing from OBS keeps your browser sources even on a fresh PC (they appear once the web engine is downloaded)
Deleting a scene or source asks first (hold Shift to skip)
The Studio engine download continues where it stopped and retries by itself, on any page
Saving scenes no longer stutters while you drag things around
Wolf Den overlays imported from OBS are drawn by Tandem itself: less than half the CPU, a third of the memory
Imported OBS cameras keep their framing at any camera mode, in every scene; OBS imports are cleared once more so you can import again fresh
If your camera is being used by another app, Tandem tells you instead of showing a blank picture
# Privacy and security
Your microphone and speakers are released a few seconds after you leave a room, and "share PC audio" never carries into the next room
Private calls option (Voice settings): calls go through Tandem's relay so nobody sees your IP address
Logins expire after 60 days unused, and signing out signs that login out everywhere
Only people actually in your room can set up a call with you; many more protections against misbehaving servers and peers
Pictures and files from the server can't be saved outside Tandem's own folder; more risky file types ask before opening
# Reliability
If this PC's graphics driver can't open Tandem's window, Tandem now says why and how to fix it (instead of not opening)
Messages sent while the connection drops are sent again after reconnecting, never twice
Your unsent text stays in the channel you typed it in
Studio sources that can't load right now (a missing plugin or device) are kept instead of being erased
Typing words that start with accented letters or emoji no longer breaks the chat
