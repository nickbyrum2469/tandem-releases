# Studio
Smoother, sharper webcam: when your camera can do 60 fps at your canvas size, Tandem uses that instead of a bigger size at 30 fps (much less motion blur)
Imported scenes no longer show a copy of Tandem inside your screen capture: Tandem hides its own window from captures (turn it off in Studio settings if you want Tandem visible on stream)
Everything you import from OBS can now be clicked, moved, resized, cropped, renamed, hidden, locked and given filters, including nested scenes and the Face Tracker's settings
# Camera picture
New Picture... button for webcams: exposure, gain, brightness, contrast, sharpness, white balance, anti-flicker and more, each with Auto, straight on the camera
One-click "Sharp motion (low light)" preset: less grain and less smearing when you move
Your camera settings are saved and put back every time the camera starts
