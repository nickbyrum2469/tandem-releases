# New: like Discord
Direct messages and group chats (click someone's name, then Message)
Reactions with colour emoji, threads, and markdown (bold, italic, code, quotes, spoilers)
Chat beside the call, real search across every message, and Windows notifications for DMs and mentions with per-channel mute and do not disturb
Roles: owner, admin and member, with kick and ban; rename spaces and channels
Profiles: picture, display name, status (online, idle, do not disturb, invisible), custom status and bio
Soundboard: short clips played into the call, with volume and per-person mute
Change your password and sign out other devices
# Calls
Video now goes through Tandem's own encoder: your picture reaches friends far sooner (about 3 ms in the encoder on AMD, was 70-130 ms)
Share screen button: show a screen or window to the call without touching your Studio scenes
Smarter video on weak connections: reacts to rising delay, paces packets, no more stuck low quality, no pointless restarts
Voice: settings apply right away, the leveler no longer over-boosts, echo cancellation judged by where sound really plays, your mic and headset found even when Windows renumbers them, clean mute, and a friend reconnecting no longer stalls everyone's voice
Redundant voice now really works for bursty connections
# Lighter
The Studio only starts when you use it, lets go of the microphone when resting, and sizes your camera to what's actually sent
Tandem rests properly when idle and runs in efficiency mode in the background
Game Capture uses the anti-cheat-safe hook; streaming shows real errors and stream health; your stream key is stored encrypted
# Easier
One Settings window for everything (gear, logo menu or Ctrl+,)
Closing Tandem during a call asks first (or keeps it running in the tray)
Better contrast on every theme, keyboard focus, screen-reader labels, and a text size setting
Remembers the window's size and place; a mic test outside calls; tidier small-window layout
# Safer
Updates roll back by themselves if a new version won't start
Messages the server refused can be sent again
Sign-in can't be locked by strangers; old copies of Tandem are only updated with signed builds
