# Rooms
Video now uses your graphics card (AMD, NVIDIA or Intel): sharp up to your full Studio size at 60 fps
Much less delay: your own tile is instant, the graphics card encodes in about 40 ms, and friends' video always shows the newest picture instead of falling behind
Colours in rooms now match the Studio
"Sending…" button in a room: pick resolution, frame rate and quality of what friends see
Video recovers from lost packets in a fraction of a second instead of freezing
Calls reconnect by themselves after a Wi-Fi drop or if someone restarts Tandem
# Voice
Echo cancellation: friends no longer hear themselves when you use speakers
Noise suppression and automatic volume
Choose your microphone and speakers (Voice settings in the room, or the TANDEM menu)
Push-to-talk on any key or mouse side button, works while you're in a game
A soft sound when people join or leave your room
# Studio
Your webcam starts with the same setup as OBS (resolution, format, filters) if OBS has it
Camera… settings: resolution, frame rate, format and your camera's own settings window
Filters: Crop/Pad, Color Correction, Color Look (LUT), Chroma Key (green screen), Luma Key, Sharpen
One source can be in many scenes: the same webcam is just "Webcam" in every scene, with different filters per scene
Drag any handle to resize without stretching; Alt+drag to crop
Change the canvas size even while you're on camera in a room
# Everything else
New app icon
Problem reports: if something breaks, Tandem can send a report to your server's host (you choose)
Safer servers: limits on password guessing, sign-ups and spam
Updates only ever move forward (an older release is never offered)
Lots of fixes from a full review of the app
