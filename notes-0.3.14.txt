# Fixes
Stopping Share audio no longer crashes Tandem
Pictures in chat open when you click them again
Save as can never delete a file you already had, even if saving fails
Being in a room on one PC no longer opens the microphone on your other PC or phone
Pictures whose message didn't go through are sent again, or shown as not delivered, instead of disappearing
# New
Your camera is ready as soon as Tandem opens, even in Chat: join a call with video without visiting the Studio
Camera button at the top right (both tabs): switch your cameras off (light goes out) or back on
Picture viewer: zoom with the mouse wheel or a double-click, drag to look around, and copy the picture (button, Ctrl+C, or right-click > Copy picture)
# Smoother video
Video timing is exact now (it slowly drifted before)
The small stream for friends who show you small uses the right bitrate
