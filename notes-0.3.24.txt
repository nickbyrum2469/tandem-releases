# A fresh new look
A calm new background like the concept art, with a slow, gentle glow (it holds still when Tandem is in the background, and follows Windows' "Show animations" setting)
Tandem's own title bar buttons: minimise, maximise, a new full screen button and close, plus the side panel and menu buttons, all drawn to match the app
Rounded window corners, glass panels, softer avatars and the same rounded shapes everywhere
The Studio now uses your theme colours instead of grey
# Smoother calls
No more picture freezes when someone joins a call (it used to restart your video up to 6 times)
Video that arrives in bursts now plays smoothly instead of skipping frames
Cameras with a face tracker or zoom filter no longer run at full size when shown small (less CPU)
Call logs are sent right after the call, and they now say whether a friend is connected directly or through the relay
Test runs no longer send fake "closed unexpectedly" reports
