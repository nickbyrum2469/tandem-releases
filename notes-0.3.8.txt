# Import from OBS
New: bring your whole OBS setup into Tandem with one click: scenes, sources, camera settings, positions, crops, filters, overlays and transitions
Find it at the top right of Studio ("Import from OBS..."); Tandem also offers it the first time you open Studio if you have OBS scenes. Your OBS files are never changed
# Video in calls
Fixed video slowing to a frame every few seconds: Tandem now adjusts video quality gently instead of every 2 seconds
# Problem reports
After each call Tandem sends its call log to Tandem cloud (only if you said yes to sharing reports), so call problems can be fixed without you having to report them
