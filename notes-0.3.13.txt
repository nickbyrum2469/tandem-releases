# Much lighter calls
Video is now sent, received and drawn on your graphics card: about a third of the processor it used before in a 1080p60 call
Your friends' video is decoded by your graphics card (same picture, pixel for pixel)
Tandem only sends your video as big as your friends actually show you, and switches up within a second when they make you bigger or full screen
If your camera only gives 30 fps, Tandem sends 30 instead of repeating every frame (overlays that move every frame stay at 60)
Nothing is sent while you're alone in a room
In bigger calls, friends who show you small get a small stream while anyone who makes you big gets the full one
# Lighter Studio
When the Studio isn't on screen and nothing is recording, streaming or sending it, it stops drawing your scenes
