# Welcome to auto-updates
- Tandem now updates itself. When a new version is out you'll see what changed and can update, skip, or wait.
- Updates are signed: Tandem only installs files signed with the Tandem release key.
# Voice & video rooms
- Join a voice room and you'll hear everyone; turn your camera on to stream your Studio scene live.
- Click a person's card to make it big; click again to go back to the grid.
- Right-click someone's card to change their volume.
- Rooms start with your camera and mic off, and everything turns off when you leave.
# Studio
- Drag edges to stretch one side, Alt-drag to crop, corners keep the shape (Shift to free it).
- If you see a resize arrow, dragging resizes — it won't move the source by accident.
- Pick a canvas size, including vertical 1080x1920.
# Fixes
- You stay logged in after closing Tandem.
