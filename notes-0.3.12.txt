# Share audio
New button in the call bar: share what your PC is playing (a video, music, a game) with everyone in the room, even while your mic is muted
Friends don't hear their own voices back, and shared audio is sent in music quality
# Smoother Studio
Web overlays and particle effects run at a smooth 60 fps again (they were stepping at around 20), using about half the processor
# Fixes
Tandem shows up in screenshots and screen shares again; hiding it from captures is now an option you can turn on in Studio settings
The Sharp motion camera preset keeps white balance on auto, so your camera can't turn pink when the room's light changes
Camera filters now apply to cameras shown inside nested scenes too (like your [Cam] scenes)
Chat stays on the newest message when you resize the window, and a cleaner welcome screen
