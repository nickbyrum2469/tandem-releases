# Smart focus for webcams
Your camera's focus stays locked (no more hunting back and forth), and refocuses on you once, by itself, only when you go soft or move closer or further away
"Focus on me" button in the Picture... window focuses on you right now and locks it
The Picture... window shows what smart focus is doing; turn it off there if you like
Uses almost nothing: about 0.1% of the processor and under 1 MB of memory, and nothing at all when it's off
