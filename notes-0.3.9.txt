# Much lighter and faster
Tandem now uses far less of your PC: about 80 MB of memory with Chat open (was 225 MB) and almost no CPU while idle (it used a whole processor core before)
With the Studio running it idles around 180 MB, less than OBS on its own
# Photos and files in chat
Paste a screenshot or picture with Ctrl+V, drag files onto the window, or press + in the message box
Pictures show right in the chat (click to view full size); other files show as a card you can download or open, up to 2 GB
# Chat
Reply to messages, @mention friends (highlighted for them), clickable links and emoji shortcodes like :fire:
Cleaner message list with day dividers, hover actions and smoother scrolling
# Import from OBS, now complete
The Face Tracker, OBS scripts and hotkeys now come over too
Your earlier import is cleared once on this update, so you can import fresh with everything included
# Studio
Cleaner Sources list, simpler Add source, and live level meters in the audio mixer
Sharper scaling of sources, and web overlays drawn on your graphics card
# Calls
Video adapts more smoothly to your connection and drops to 1080p or 720p when it can't carry more (sharper than a blurry 1440p)
Each friend's video shows connection bars; hover to see their resolution, frame rate and packet loss
The Sending button turns amber when your connection is limiting what you send
