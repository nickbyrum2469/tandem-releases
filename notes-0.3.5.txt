# Tandem cloud
Tandem now has its own always-on server: sign in, create spaces and chat even when nobody's PC is hosting
Everything over the internet is encrypted, and Tandem checks it's really talking to the right server
# Invites
"Invite friends" now copies an invite link; paste it into Tandem (on the sign-in screen or in Join space) to join that space, even on another server
Your friend's Tandem signs in or creates an account on the right server for them, then joins the space
# Calls
Voice and video calls now connect even on home and mobile internet that blocks direct connections, through Tandem's call relay
# Hosting on your own PC
Hosting now opens your router's port automatically (UPnP) and closes it again when Tandem exits
