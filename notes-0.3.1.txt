# A fresh new look
New typeface (Inter) and a calmer, darker design across the whole app
Studio and Chat now share one look: teal accents, soft dividers, rounded cards
# Chat
Cleaner sidebar: channels and rooms are proper lists with icons
Voice rooms show who's inside with small mic and camera icons
Clicking a voice room opens a lobby showing who's there, with a Join button
"Invite friends" right at the top of every space; space menu under its name
Messages are better aligned, with quieter timestamps
Floating call controls and nicer video tiles in rooms
New sign-in screen
# Studio
Scenes are a tidy list; move/delete buttons appear when you hover a scene
Calmer status bar and docks
