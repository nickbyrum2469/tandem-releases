# Game mode
While a game is running, Tandem steps back so the game gets your PC first: lower priority for its work and its graphics (your voice keeps its own fast lane)
When a game covers Tandem, friends' video pauses (voice carries on) and comes back the moment you switch to Tandem; on another monitor it's lighter (360p, 30 fps)
Your camera is sent at 720p 30 fps at most while you play, and the face tracker and background removal pause (never while recording or streaming); "Keep full quality while gaming" in the Sending menu turns this off
The Studio runs at 30 fps while you play, and only opens cameras that a scene on screen or in the call shows
Tandem no longer puts its graphics work ahead of your game (the Studio engine used to ask Windows for high graphics priority)
Friends' video uses less memory and processor time on every PC
Only one Tandem runs at a time: opening it again brings the open one to the front
# Clearer voices
A late piece of someone's voice is filled in smoothly instead of becoming a hole, and playback adjusts its speed very slightly to stay steady
Echo cancellation is now Automatic: off on headsets (where it only thinned voices), on for speakers
No more crackle when two people talk at once or someone is turned up: a soft limiter instead of a hard cut, and a "your microphone is too loud" hint
Your voice only carries a backup copy when packets are actually being lost, so more of it goes to sound quality
When Windows changes its default device (a controller connects), Tandem asks before switching instead of jumping to it
Voice settings shows each microphone's quality, with a note for phone-quality ones; a mic with one silent side uses the other
New "Strong" noise removal for keyboards and fans
# Calls
Sharing your PC's audio no longer sends your mic twice with SteelSeries Sonar (it now shares only Sonar's Game and Media channels)
Right-click a friend: separate volume for their voice and for the audio they share, so you can turn their video down and still hear them talk
# Studio
"Hide Tandem from screen captures" is now Never for everyone (you can still pick Automatic or Always)
OBS import: web page sources get a "Restart Tandem now" button instead of waiting for you to restart
OBS import: a clip or picture with no file in OBS, and Wolf Den chat alerts waiting for an alert, are no longer called "not working"
Call logs keep their important parts instead of being cut off
