# A cleaner window
Tandem's own title bar: minimise, full screen and close in Tandem's look; drag to move, double-click to fill the screen, F11 for full screen, resize from any edge
The side panel folds to icons (button at the top left) and you can drag its edge wider or narrower; Tandem remembers
In a call and looking at something else? "V1 · 2 inside · Back to call" at the top takes you straight back
Hover over buttons, rooms and pages to see what they do
Your Studio's screen capture no longer films Tandem itself (no more endless mirror of the window)
# Calls that just work
Joining a room never mutes you, and your camera comes on the way you last had it; no trip to the Studio needed
Deafen mutes you too, and undeafening unmutes you again, but only if deafen is what muted you; you can unmute while staying deafened
# Voice as good as Discord
Headset mics that send low-quality sound (many wireless and Bluetooth headsets) are now converted cleanly instead of sounding metallic
Automatic gain really works now (it was switched on but did nothing): quiet voices are levelled up
Voice keeps its full range, with a filter for breathing, desk knocks and hum; voice is sent at 64 kbps like Discord
Unplugging or changing your headset mid-call switches over by itself
# Sharper, lighter video
Video gets smaller within 2 seconds when someone shrinks your tile (was 10), and comes back at once when they enlarge it
A friend's old 10 or 20 Mbps video setting goes back to Auto: far less to download, far smoother on both PCs
After a size change, video starts at the right quality for that size instead of looking soft for a minute
A call you're not looking at for a while gets smaller video, until you look again
New "Friends' video: up to 720p / 1080p" choice in the video quality menu
Heavy packet loss drops straight to a safe rate; calls with three or more people start smaller so nobody's connection collapses
Fewer video restarts, so fewer glitches
# Sounds
New soft sounds for join, leave and mentions, plus mute, unmute, deafen, undeafen and "connection lost"
Settings (click the Tandem logo) -> Sounds: Tandem's sounds, Jayton's sounds, or your own WAV/MP3 files
# Studio
Your camera runs only at the size it's shown at, and the Studio rests when your camera is off in a call
Plugins you installed into OBS (shader filters, face tracker, freeze and more) are loaded from your OBS so imports keep them
Imports say honestly what came over and what needs a plugin; nothing missing is thrown away
Imported mics that don't exist on this PC use your default mic; local web overlays load; a webcam that fails to start is tried again
Smart focus no longer keeps refocusing
